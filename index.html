Kaféra

Mobile coffee & beverage catering

Bring the café experience to your venue. Professional baristas serve handcrafted drinks, made from moka pot coffee, at birthdays, weddings, executive meetings, appreciation events and corporate functions.

Kaféra started in January 2026 after our founder completed TESDA National Certificate II in Barista, and a 50th birthday celebration became its first event.

100 and 200 cupsExpanded beverage selection, longer service, event customization.

Service coverage includes location, travel arrangements, event requirements and venue preparation. Quoted per event.

Anne Émeraude

Signature fragrance souvenirs

Handcrafted room and linen sprays, designed as elegant event souvenirs, appreciation gifts and premium keepsakes. Each one can carry names, event dates, logos, colors or custom packaging.

Anne Émeraude began as a personal experiment in making a signature scent. When someone asked for custom perfume souvenirs for an 18th birthday, it became a collection about memories and meaningful occasions.

Every fragrance is aged for at least two weeks after the materials arrive, so the scent can fully develop. Good for birthday souvenirs, corporate gifts, wedding tokens and personalized gifts, with a choice of bottles and packaging styles.

How we work together

Seven clear steps, from your first message to the day of your event.

  1. Inquiry

    Share your event details, preferred products or ideas.

  2. Consultation

    We learn your vision, budget and timeline, recommend options, and send a detailed quotation.

  3. Confirmation

    A 50% down payment confirms your order. Preparation starts after it is received.

  4. Production

    Beverage planning, merchandise production, or fragrance preparation and aging.

  5. Quality check

    Everything is checked for presentation and function before release.

  6. Final payment

    The balance is settled before release, delivery or event service.

  7. Delivery

    We deliver your order, or bring the experience to your event.

Lead times: merchandise 3 to 4 weeks, fragrances at least 2 weeks of aging after materials arrive. Contact us early for your date.
